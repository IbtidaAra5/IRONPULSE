Privacy Policy

Welcome to the IronPulse Gym Management System. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect your personal information. By using our services you agree to these practices.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: name, email, phone number and contact details given at registration.
  • Account Information: username and (hashed) password.
  • Fitness Data: height, weight, BMI, body measurements, workout and diet plans, and progress records.
  • Usage Data: attendance, trainer assignments and features you use.
  • Payment Information: membership payment records needed to process and track billing.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To manage memberships, attendance, trainers and plans.
  • To send reminders such as membership expiry and payment dues.
  • To improve our services and ensure system security.

3. Data Security

We use hashed passwords, role-based access control and restricted staff access to protect your data. No system is completely secure, but we work to reduce risk.

4. Data Retention

We keep your information while your membership is active and as long as required by law or for record keeping.

5. Your Rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of your personal data, or object to certain uses, by contacting us.

6. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your data. We may share it with payment or messaging providers that help deliver our services, or when required by law.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.

8. Contact Us

Questions? Email privacy@ironpulse.com.
